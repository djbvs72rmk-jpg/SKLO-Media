Independent by design.

The family connects products through explicit interfaces. It does not require a shared codebase, runtime or release cycle.

A purpose for each product

Use an individual tool for its task. Family membership does not create a mandatory dependency on every other tool.

Integration through contracts

Shared results and interfaces are defined explicitly, rather than inferred from internal files or shared storage.

Honest about readiness

Planned capabilities, working tools and public releases are different things. Product pages describe their own boundaries.
