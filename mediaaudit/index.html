MediaAudit rclone

MediaAudit rclone is a private, personal-use integration used by its owner to synchronize the owner's MediaAudit files between Google Drive and the owner's server.

Purpose

The integration uses Google Drive only as a storage and transfer channel for MediaAudit distribution files, completed results, handoff evidence, and related metadata.

Access

The integration is intended for the owner's own Google account and server. It is not offered as a public service and does not provide accounts to third parties.

Data handling

Google Drive data accessed by the integration is used only to perform the owner's requested synchronization workflow. The integration does not sell user data, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties.
