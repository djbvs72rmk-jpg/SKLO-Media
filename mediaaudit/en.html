Ukrainian name: Призма. Formerly MediaAudit ToolKit.

One source. Several perspectives.

01 / PRYZMA

Look beneath the surface.

Pryzma is a tool for controlled video and audio analysis. Technical checks, recognized text and audit results help inform the next decision about your material.

01

Technical analysis

Inspect the technical properties of video and audio and produce structured results.

02

Speech and text

Speech recognition, text outputs and subtitles within supported workflows.

03

Quality control

A separate audit of the final edited master. Automated checks do not replace the final human decision.

You stay in control.

Source-media analysis and final-master review are separate workflows. The required checkpoints between stages remain in place.

  • Recognized words do not guarantee correct semantic understanding.
  • Automatic subtitles require human review.
  • A problem report is not permission to delete or replace an original.

Personal Google Drive integration

MediaAudit rclone remains the name of the personal integration that transfers product files between the owner’s Google Drive and server. It is not a public service and does not offer third-party accounts.

Google Drive is a storage and transfer channel for distribution files, completed results and related metadata. Renaming the product on this page does not change the OAuth application name, permissions or configuration.
