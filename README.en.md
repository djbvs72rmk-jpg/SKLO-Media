**SKLO** is the umbrella brand. **Media** is the product family. **Pryzma**, **Atlas** and **MediaOrder** are individual products with their own purpose and development.

This repository brings public descriptions, documentation and integration information together. It does not turn the products into one application, database, repository or server.

## Family principles

**Independent development.** Each product retains its own ownership, acceptance and release history.

**Integration through explicit contracts.** Sharing results does not imply merging repositories, databases or runtimes.

**Clear boundaries.** Planned capabilities are not presented as releases. Private data, media and secrets are not published.
